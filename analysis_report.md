**Target State:**
- Target Score: 10/10 (AI Overview SEO Ready)

**Required Sections to Add:**
* **Direct Answer Box (40-60 words):** Placed immediately after H1 for featured snippet capture. Bold key terms.

* **Main content sections (expanded):** Deep dives into the core topic.

* **"Quick Action Plan" section:** (200-250 words) Numbered, actionable steps.
* **"When to See a Dentist" section:** (150-200 words) Urgency levels and professional advice.
* **"Signs You Should Not Ignore" section:** (100-150 words) Bulleted red flags.
* **5 High-Intent FAQs:** (2-3 lines each) Formatted for People Also Ask schema.
* **Conclusion:** (100-150 words) Clear CTA.

**Trust Signals & Readability:**
* **Internal linking:** 3-5 contextual links naturally woven into content (targeting `/tools/gum-disease-risk-calculator` and other pillars).
* **Trust signals:** Add Author Section (Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS - 50-75 words), Expert Insight quotes, Real-life Examples (100-150 words), Medical Disclaimer, Affiliate Disclaimer.
* **Readability:** Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max), bolded terms for emphasis, heavy use of bullet points.
